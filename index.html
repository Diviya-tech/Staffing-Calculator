Service Desk Staffing Calculator

Type a start date and the calculator forecasts the phone calls from earlier years, then shows how many people to schedule in each hour. You can also type your own contact numbers on the other tabs. The calculator shows how many people to put on the phones so that 80% of contacts are answered within 30 seconds, and what the wait becomes if you staff less. The example numbers on each tab are placeholders until you type your own.

Semester start dates used

Earlier years use the first Monday of each semester in the Block 4 windows. The 2027 dates follow the same pattern (Spring 2027 is listed by the university as starting Monday 11 January 2027; the Fall 2027 date is our estimate). Replace them with the official dates.

How it works: for each day it takes the same weekday in the same week of the semester in the last few years, averages the phone tickets, multiplies by calls per ticket (1.65, measured from PureCloud), spreads the calls over the hours of the day using the usual hourly shape for that semester, then asks the Erlang model how many people each hour needs. It cannot see one-off events, enrolment changes or outages. All numbers are rounded to whole calls and whole people.

The effect depends on how much of the phone coverage students already provide. Students covered 0% to 14% of phone capacity before 2026 and 30% to 40% in Spring and Fall 2026, so the latest two rows are the best guide. The 25% is added in every hour.

Question 2. How many student employees for 2027?

These are student counts for the five busiest weeks of each semester (two weeks before classes start through week 3), at 19 hours per student per week, after subtracting the full-time and part-time coverage seen in the latest semester. Outside those weeks the need is much lower, so hours per student can be cut after week 3.

Read these numbers as a floor. Checked against PureCloud 2026, the model said 60% of calls were answered within 30 seconds, while PureCloud reported about 34%. Real staffing needs are therefore higher than shown. Daily service level from Genesys would let us close that gap.

What each number rests on
  • Demand: daily phone tickets from ServiceNow (2022 to 2026), averaged over the last three Fall or Spring windows, times 1.65 calls per ticket (PureCloud phone offers divided by in-scope ServiceNow phone tickets, Dec 2025 to Sep 2026).
  • Supply: When I Work shifts, split into full-time, part-time and student, by hour. A person is on the queue for 70% of a scheduled hour. A student on a student-only queue counts half as much on the phones as a person on all queues.
  • Handle time: PureCloud shows about 8 minutes (469 to 562 seconds). The client quoted 15 minutes, so both are shown.
  • Callers hang up after about 15 minutes on average, worked out from the real abandon rate and wait times.
  • Not yet known: which positions take phone calls, the true share of student hours on phones, and semester start dates for 2027. Those are open questions for the client.
